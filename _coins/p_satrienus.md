Few images say "Rome" like the wolf, yet this is not the nurturing she-wolf of Romulus, but the aggressive and fierce Capitoline wolf of a dominant Rome. Issued in the aftermath of the Social War, when Rome's Italian allies rebelled against her, this denarius trumpets Rome's restored mastery over them. The obverse is typically described as Roma, but an obverse of Mars is also likely. The god of war and the symbol of Rome triumphant, along with the name ROMA as masters of Italy, all in one coin.
